Explain how the water cycle works to a curious beginner in India, in English.
Target 60–90 seconds, about 155–180 spoken words across seven short scenes.

Reference notes:
- Solar energy supports evaporation of surface water; evaporation does not require boiling.
- Plants also release water vapour through transpiration.
- Cooling moist air can cause vapour to condense on particles into droplets or ice crystals.
- Water returns as precipitation, including rain, snow and hail.
- Water can flow over land, infiltrate the ground, or be stored before returning to circulation.
- Gravity drives precipitation and downhill flow; the sun supplies energy for evaporation.
- Avoid suggesting that clouds consist of gaseous water vapour or that every drop follows one identical route.

Use the available overview, evaporation, condensation, rain, and collection scene types.
