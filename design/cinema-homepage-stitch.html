Clear Answers

Frequently asked questions.

What length and languages are supported?

+
NamasteVideo generates 60–90 second explainer videos in English. This format is optimized for vertical platforms like Reels and YouTube Shorts, keeping explanations crisp, paced, and engaging.

Can I edit the storyboard and captions before rendering?

+
Yes, absolutely. The storyboard stage is an explicit review gate. You can edit voiceover narration, modify caption wording, or ask the AI to re-simplify any scene before final video compilation.

Can I download the video without connecting an Instagram account?

+
Yes. Every rendered video is immediately available as a standard 1080p MP4 download file. You can save it to your device and use it anywhere without ever connecting Instagram.

What account is required to publish directly to Instagram?

+
Direct publishing requires an eligible Instagram Professional (Business or Creator) account. Personal Instagram accounts are not supported by Instagram's API for external publishing.

Who can access the platform right now?

+
NamasteVideo.ai is currently in an internal preview phase for approved creators and educators. Access is provided via sign-in for authenticated preview users.
