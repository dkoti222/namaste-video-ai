The three previous directions were rejected and deleted, including their Stitch project. This document replaces them. Product specifications and approved video prototypes remain the source of truth for behavior and video output.

## Reference

Reference: https://namastedev.com/ — inspected in the browser.

Observed: near-black background (`#030303` on the body), warm orange (`#E58C33` on links and accents), white headings, gray supporting text, understated perspective grid, Plus Jakarta Sans hero typography and Inter supporting text. ## Shared design rules

- Use orange sparingly: primary action, active step, selected state, one headline fragment. Neutral surfaces should dominate.
- Orange buttons use near-black labels. Do not use white text on orange for ordinary button labels.
- Body copy 16px/24px; field labels 14px/20px; metadata 12–13px used sparingly. Heading scale 24/32/40/56px; large marketing specimens can use 64px.
- Use a 4px spacing base, predominantly 8/16/24/32/48/64px. Controls 44px minimum height, corner radius 8px; video frames and major panels 16px.
- Use consistent outline icons, 20–24px, with text labels for important actions. Avoid decorative AI sparkles.
- Text and essential control boundaries must meet WCAG AA contrast in implementation; muted dividers must not be the sole input boundary. Use a clearly contrasting orange focus ring with an offset.
- Pair status colors with text/icon labels. Orange is the brand/action color, not automatically an error or warning.
- Keep one primary action in each step. Preserve Idea → Storyboard → Video → Publish. Conversational editing is integrated into the active stage.
- Motion: short 160–240ms state changes. The homepage hero may use a purposeful 8-second silent demonstration, with pause/replay controls and a static reduced-motion alternative. Avoid unrelated perpetual ambient movement. The product video player has separate playback controls.
- Use actual video/diagram examples, clear titles and honest progress. Avoid decorative metrics, invented social proof, billing controls, or shared-team UI.
- Product chrome and generated-video styling are separate systems. This exploration does not recolor the previously approved videos.
